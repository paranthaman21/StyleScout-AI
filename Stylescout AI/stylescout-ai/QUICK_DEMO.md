1. **Select your outfit style:**
   - Traditional/Ethnic
   - Formal/Business
   - Casual
   - Sporty
   - Party/Glamorous
   - Streetwear

2. **Adjust formality level:** (1-10 slider)

3. **Choose color mood:**
   - Warm
   - Cool
   - Neutral
   - Vibrant

4. **Enter location:**
   - Example: "Chennai, Tamil Nadu"

   - Photography tips
